PRIVACY

Privacy

What the office collects: nothing it does not need.

  • The site sets no cookies and runs no analytics scripts. Edge request logs are Cloudflare's, retained on Cloudflare's schedule, read by the office only as aggregate counts.
  • The records carry public-record data about legal entities and, where a register discloses them, the names of their directors or owners. No other personal data is collected, and no register field is enriched from any other source.
  • The contact form sends your email address and message to the office's form processor (Formspree) and on to the office. It is used to reply to you and for nothing else. Ask and it is deleted.
  • The paid door records the settlement receipt: transaction, payer address, amount, timestamp. Receipts are public by design — that is what a receipt is for. No name is attached to a payer address by the office.
  • Agents that authenticate to the office's authorization server are recorded by the identity they present, for the life of the token they were issued.
  • The office does not sell, share or trade any of the above.

Controller: Agentic KG Holdings, West Vancouver, British Columbia, Canada. Contact through the form on the home page.
